## 1. Multimodal representation and alignment

| Source | Claim supported | Qualification |
|---|---|---|
| [CLIP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.00020) | Trains on 400M image-caption pairs with image/text contrastive matching and demonstrates zero-shot transfer across more than 30 vision datasets. | Supports image-text alignment and zero-shot transfer, not general visual reasoning or reliable OCR in every setting. |

|---|---|---|

## 3. Diffusion and image generation

| Source | Claim supported | Qualification |
|---|---|---|
| [Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.11239) | Defines a forward Gaussian noising process and learned reverse denoising process; uses a noise-prediction objective. | Original CIFAR-10/LSUN results are historical baselines; later samplers and architectures change efficiency. |
| [Classifier-Free Diffusion Guidance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.12598) | Combines conditional and unconditional score/noise predictions to trade off prompt adherence and diversity without a separate classifier. | High guidance can cause saturation, artifacts or reduced diversity; the scale is not a monotonic quality control. |
| [Latent Diffusion Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.10752) | Performs diffusion in a pretrained autoencoder latent space and uses cross-attention for conditioning to reduce pixel-space computation. | Latent compression can discard fine detail; cost/quality depends on the autoencoder and task. |

## 6. Calculation and implementation checks

- **InfoNCE:** the lesson's displayed expression is one directional image-to-text contrastive term. Full CLIP training uses a symmetric image-to-text and text-to-image objective. Temperature τ controls logit sharpness; false negatives and duplicate captions can distort the signal.
- **ViT patches:** 224/16 gives 196 patches + CLS = 197 tokens. 384/16 gives 576 + CLS = 577; 384/8 gives 2,304 + CLS = 2,305. Patch count rises 4× and the standard global attention score matrix's (N^2) term rises about 16×.
- **DDPM:** (x_t=\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}x_0+\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}\epsilon) and the noise-prediction MSE are consistent with the original formulation.
- **CFG:** ε̂=ε_uncond+s(ε_cond-ε_uncond) is the standard classifier-free guidance combination; high s may over-amplify conditioning and reduce diversity.
- **PCM data size:** 16 kHz × 2 bytes × 60 s = 1,920,000 bytes = 1.92 MB decimal for mono; stereo doubles it. Container and codec overhead are excluded.
- **WER:** 4 substitutions + 3 deletions + 2 insertions over 100 reference words gives 9/100 = 9%.
- **Video sampling:** 1 frame/s from 30 fps samples about 1/30 of the frames; a 100 ms event can be missed. “Not sampled” must not be reported as “did not happen.”

## 7. Review conclusion

All 20 M09 source occurrences match their scoped claims. Corrections were limited to citation metadata and version status: Whisper, EnCodec and Video-LLaMA titles now match the papers; NIST Privacy Framework 1.1 is explicitly marked as a draft. Formula examples for contrastive learning, ViT patch counts, diffusion, PCM storage, WER and frame sampling were checked. The curriculum distinguishes model architecture claims from benchmark-specific performance and does not treat a shared embedding score or automated video/audio benchmark as proof of semantic truth.
